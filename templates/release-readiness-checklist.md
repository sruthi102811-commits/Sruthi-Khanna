# Release Readiness Checklist

## 1. Scope & Planning
- Release scope finalized and approved
- Objectives and success criteria defined
- Stakeholders aligned on timing and impact

## 2. Risk & Dependencies
- Top risks identified, scored, and owned
- Cross-team dependencies confirmed
- Mitigation plans documented

## 3. Quality & Validation
- Testing completed and signed off
- Security and compliance checks complete
- Performance and reliability validated

## 4. Rollback & Monitoring
- Rollback plan reviewed and tested
- Monitoring, alerts, and dashboards in place
- Support teams prepared for release

## 5. Communication
- Release notes finalized
- Stakeholders and support teams notified
- Escalation paths confirmed

## 6. Go / No-Go Decision
- Final readiness review completed
- Decision recorded with accountable owner
