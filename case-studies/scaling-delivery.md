# Scaling Delivery Across Multiple Teams

## Context
Platform program spanning **8–15 engineering and partner teams** delivering interconnected capabilities with shared infrastructure dependencies.

## Problem
- Competing priorities across teams
- Unclear ownership and sequencing
- Delivery commitments frequently at risk
- Excessive leadership time spent resolving escalations

## Approach
- Implemented dependency mapping and milestone alignment
- Established a consistent delivery cadence and reporting rhythm
- Clarified ownership, escalation paths, and decision rights
- Focused leadership conversations on outcomes and tradeoffs

## Execution
- Facilitated cross-team planning and dependency reviews
- Drove accountability for committed milestones
- Enabled teams to surface risks early and resolve blockers
- Provided leadership with concise, decision-oriented updates


## At-a-Glance

| Area | Summary |
|------|---------|
| Problem | Delivery slowed by unclear ownership, fragmented signals, and escalation-heavy coordination |
| What I did | Built a scaled delivery operating model: intake → dependency mapping → risk signals → governance cadence |
| Outcome | **+40% improvement in delivery predictability**, reduced leadership escalations, stronger execution focus |

## Lessons Learned
- Scale requires **structure, transparency, and alignment** — not micromanagement
- Shared signals outperform manual coordination
- Clear ownership reduces escalation noise
- Predictability increases executive confidence
